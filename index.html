Our services

Care that is simple to understand.

Most people find us because they need one thing quickly, then stay for the rest. Every service is under one roof, with one provider who knows your history.

Everyday & urgent care

Same-week sick visits and the everyday care that keeps you well.

  • Primary careAnnual wellness exams, preventive screenings, sick visits and the everyday care that keeps you well.
  • Urgent careSame-week visits for non-emergency illness and injury, without an ER wait or an ER bill.

Walk-ins welcome Monday through Friday, 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Saturday visits are by appointment.

Ongoing health

Long-term care for the conditions you live with, year after year.

  • Chronic disease careOngoing care for the conditions you live with, coordinated by one provider who knows you.
  • Diabetes careBlood sugar monitoring, medication management and practical education you can act on.
  • Cardiac careBlood pressure and heart health monitoring, with referral to cardiology when needed.
  • COPD & lung careBreathing assessments and long-term management for COPD, asthma and chronic lung conditions.
  • Medication managementRegular review of everything you take, to keep it working together safely.
  • Post-hospital careFollow-up care after a hospital stay, to keep recovery on track and avoid readmission.

Fast exams and in-office treatments, including DOT cards for drivers off I‑75.

  • DOT physicalsDOT medical exams for CDL drivers, usually in and out in under 30 minutes with your card in hand.

  • Drug screeningsEmployment and pre-employment drug screening for Ocala employers and individuals.
  • Wound careAssessment, cleaning, dressing and monitoring for wounds that need attention over time.
  • Joint injectionsIn-office injections for knee, shoulder and other joint pain, without a specialist referral.

Wellness

About Dr. Ricketts

Primary care from a provider who stays.

Nichola Ricketts, DNP, APRN-BC, is a board-certified family nurse practitioner and a contributing member of the Ocala community for more than 20 years.

Her focus is chronic disease management and preventive health, built on a strong background in geriatric and end-of-life care. That experience shapes how she practices: fewer assumptions, more listening, and a plan built around the patient in front of her rather than a protocol.
